Earlier rejected-source and relative-Location receipts remain historical mechanism evidence only.

Execution evidence belongs to the integration owner's observed build/pilot receipts, not this source change log. Default and production adoption remain subject to the required producer/publisher/two-path/OnixOS pilots and their exact evidence.
